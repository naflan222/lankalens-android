# LankaLens — Google Play listing copy

## App name
LankaLens

## Short description
Buy, sell and discover cameras, lenses and photography gear across Sri Lanka.

## Full description
LankaLens is a Sri Lankan camera marketplace that brings buyers, individual sellers and camera shops together in one place.

Use LankaLens to browse camera bodies, lenses, action cameras, drones and photography accessories; search and filter listings; view seller and shop information; save favourites; contact sellers; and create your own listings with photos.

Business users can maintain a shop presence and manage their marketplace listings, while individual users can list used or new camera gear for buyers across Sri Lanka.

Key features include:
- Camera and photography marketplace listings
- Search and category filters
- Product photos and listing details
- Individual seller and business shop profiles
- Favourites
- Listing creation with photo uploads
- Messaging/contact options
- Links to supported shop social profiles

Website: https://lankalens.lk
Support: support@lankalens.online
