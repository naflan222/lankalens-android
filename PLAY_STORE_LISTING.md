## Full description
LankaLens is a Sri Lankan marketplace built for photographers, creators, camera shops and anyone looking to buy or sell camera gear.

Browse cameras, lenses, action cameras, drones and photography accessories from individual sellers and verified business shops around Sri Lanka.

Key features:
- Browse camera gear and photography accessories
- Search and filter marketplace listings
- View listing photos, prices and seller information
- Create an account and post your own ads
- Upload listing photos directly from your Android phone
- Save favourite listings
- Explore camera shops and business profiles
- Contact sellers and use LankaLens messaging features
- Open supported social and contact links from seller profiles

LankaLens connects to the official LankaLens marketplace at https://lankalens.lk and keeps your marketplace experience in one dedicated Android app.
