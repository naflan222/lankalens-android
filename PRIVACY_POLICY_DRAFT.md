# LankaLens Privacy Policy — draft for review

**Status:** Draft only. Review this against the actual LankaLens backend, analytics, email, storage and payment setup before publishing.

LankaLens provides a marketplace for cameras, lenses and photography equipment in Sri Lanka.

## Information users may provide
Depending on the features used, LankaLens may process account information such as name and email address, profile information, listing details, uploaded photos, shop/business information, contact details, messages or enquiries, favourites and other marketplace activity.

## How information is used
Information is used to provide account access, display marketplace listings and shop profiles, enable buyer/seller interactions, operate favourites and messaging features, prevent abuse, maintain security, provide support and improve the service.

## Images and files
Photos uploaded for listings, profiles or businesses may be stored using LankaLens's configured storage provider and displayed to other users where the related listing or profile is public.

## Service providers
LankaLens may use hosting, database, email, file-storage and other infrastructure providers to operate the service. Information is shared with those providers only as necessary to provide the relevant service, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

## Security
LankaLens uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect account and marketplace information. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

## Data retention and deletion
Information may be retained while an account, listing or business profile is active and for a reasonable period afterward where needed for security, support, legal or operational purposes. Users should be given a method to request account/data deletion where required by the service and applicable rules.

## Children
LankaLens should be used only by users who meet the age requirements stated in the service terms and applicable law. Final Play Console target-audience answers must match the actual product rules.

## Changes
This policy may be updated when LankaLens features, providers or legal requirements change.

Support: support@lankalens.online
Website: https://lankalens.lk
