LankaLens provides a marketplace where people in Sri Lanka can browse, buy and sell cameras, lenses, action cameras, drones and photography accessories. The Android app connects to the LankaLens service at https://lankalens.lk.

## Information users provide
Depending on the features used, LankaLens may process information users submit to the service, such as:
- account information, including name and email address;
- profile or business/shop information;
- marketplace listings, descriptions, prices and uploaded images;
- favourites, offers and other marketplace activity;
- messages sent through LankaLens features;
- contact or social-profile information a user chooses to add.

## Information processed by the Android app
The Android app requires internet access to load and use LankaLens. When a user chooses photos for a listing or profile, the app processes the selected images on the device and may resize/compress them before upload. The app does not request general access to all device photos; users choose the files they want to provide through Android's file picker.

Normal technical information may also be processed when connecting to the service, such as IP address, browser/WebView information, timestamps and security or diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the service.

## How information is used
Information may be used to:
- provide accounts and marketplace features;
- publish and display user-created listings and shop profiles;
- enable communication and marketplace interactions;
- maintain security, prevent abuse and investigate technical problems;
- operate, improve and support LankaLens.
